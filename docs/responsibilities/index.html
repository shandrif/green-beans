Green Beans · Sample to PO

Department responsibilities
& process handover

How every green bean sample moves from Procurement to the Purchase Order, who owns each step, and what each team records in the Green Beans app.

Half Million Coffee · Internal process documentApp: beans.hmreport.com

1Department responsibilities

Procurement

Steps 1, 5, 6, 8
  1. Receives or sources the green bean sample from the supplier.
  2. Registers the sample using its existing sample ID and sends it to Roasting.
  3. After Roasting and NPD approvals, negotiates and confirms the supplier.
  4. Asks Warehouse to create the PR.
  5. Creates the PO in Oracle using the confirmed terms and the PR, then records it in the app.
Records in the app

Supplier, origin, variety, process, grade, lot, sample weight, indicative price · agreed price, quantity, Incoterms, payment terms, delivery window · PR request · PO number, date and value.

Handover: sample to Roasting · PR request to Warehouse · PO recorded and handed over to Oracle.

Roasting

Steps 2–3
  1. Receives the sample from Procurement (it appears in the Roasting queue).
  2. Roasts and evaluates it over about 2–3 days.
  3. Checks the bean meets the company's quality requirements.
  4. Records the evaluation and approves or rejects the sample.
Records in the app

Received and roast dates, roast profile, Agtron, moisture %, water activity, density, screen size, primary and secondary defects, cupping score, flavour and evaluation notes.

Handover: approved → moves to NPD automatically · rejected → closed with a required reason visible to Procurement.

NPD · New Product Development

Step 4
  1. Receives only samples approved by Roasting.
  2. Checks the bean fits a product or blend; cupping is optional.
  3. Records its decision and comments.
Records in the app

Target product or blend, intended use, product fit, cupping done (yes / no), cupping date and score, sensory notes, comments.

Handover: approved → Procurement starts negotiation · rejected → closed with a required reason.

Warehouse

Step 7
  1. Receives the PR request from Procurement after approvals and supplier confirmation.
  2. Creates the Purchase Requisition (PR) in Oracle for the expected stock.
  3. Records the PR reference in the app for Procurement.
Records in the app

PR number, PR date, PR quantity (kg), storage location, notes.

Handover: PR details sent to Procurement to create the PO.

3Key handover rules

Roasting approval before NPDOnly samples approved by Roasting reach NPD.
NPD approval before negotiationProcurement negotiates only after both Roasting and NPD approve.
Supplier confirmation before the PRWarehouse is asked for a PR only after the supplier is confirmed.
Each team fills only its own stepsStages open in order; other teams can view but not edit them.
PR and PO are created in OracleWarehouse owns the PR, Procurement owns the PO. The app records their references; there is no automatic Oracle link.
Rejection and resubmissionA rejection needs a reason and closes the sample. Procurement can resubmit a new sample from the same supplier, linked to the original.
